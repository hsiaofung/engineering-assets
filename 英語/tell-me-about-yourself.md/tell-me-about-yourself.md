# tell me about + 主題: 
1. tell me about yourself. 母語人士自我介紹的慣用說法。

   - 語塊: tell me about + 主題
   - 例如: 
        - tell me about your job.
        - tell me about your family.
        - tell me about your experience.

# 自我介紹(Self-Introduction): 

| 項目 | 非正式 Informal | 正式 Formal |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Greeting** 問候 | Hi! / Hello! | Good morning. / Good afternoon. / Good evening. |
| **2. Name** 姓名 | I'm Tim. | My name is Tim. It's a pleasure to meet you. |
| **3. Where are you from?** 來自哪裡 | I'm from Taiwan. | I'm originally from Taiwan. |
| **4. What do you do?** 職業 | I work as a software engineer. | I'm a software engineer at Supermicro. |
| **5. Who do you live with?** 家庭 | I live with my wife and two sons. | I live with my wife and our two sons. |
| **6. Do you have any brothers or sisters?** 兄弟姊妹 | I have one older brother and one older sister. | I have an older brother and an older sister. |
| **7. Hobbies** 興趣 | In my free time, I like watching YouTube. I go cycling on weekends. I'm a big fan of Jay Chou. | In my spare time, I enjoy watching YouTube and cycling. I'm also a big fan of Jay Chou. |
| **8. Daily Routine** 日常作息 | On weekdays, I usually get up at 7:00, buy breakfast, and go to work. In the morning, I have breakfast at work and start work at 9:00. After work, I usually pick up dinner on my way home. In the evening, I have dinner and watch YouTube. | On weekdays, I usually wake up at 7:00 a.m. and head to work after buying breakfast. I usually have breakfast at work before starting my workday at 9:00 a.m. After work, I pick up dinner on my way home. In the evening, I enjoy dinner with my family and watch YouTube. |
| **9. Personality** 個性 | I'm friendly and curious. | I would describe myself as friendly and curious. |
| **10. Future Plans** 未來計畫 | My goal is to speak English more confidently at work. In the future, I'd like to travel and meet people from different countries. | One of my goals is to improve my English communication skills and become more confident when speaking English at work. In the future, I hope to travel more and meet people from different cultures. |
